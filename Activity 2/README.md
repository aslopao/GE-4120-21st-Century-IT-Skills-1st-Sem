## 📘 ACTIVITY TITLE AND SHORT DESCRIPTION

**Activity 02: Personal Branding**

<p align="justify">
This activity focuses on developing a personal brand through creative visual elements such as colors, typography, layouts, and design choices that represent my personality, interests, and unique identity. It explores how visual presentation can communicate who I am and how I want others to perceive me. Through this activity, I was able to express my individuality while learning how to create a consistent and meaningful personal identity through digital design.
</p>

## ✨ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

<p align="justify">
Through this activity, I learned that personal branding is more than just creating an attractive design. It is about presenting my identity, personality, interests, and strengths in a way that makes me unique and recognizable. I realized that the visual elements I choose can communicate something about who I am even without using many words.
</p>

**◆ Key Concepts and Skills I Gained**

<p align="justify">
I learned how to use colors, typography, visual elements, and layouts to create a consistent personal identity. I also developed my creativity and gained a better understanding of how visual design can communicate personality and individuality. This activity helped me become more aware of the importance of presenting myself confidently and professionally.
</p>

**◆ Challenges I Faced and How I Solved Them**

<p align="justify">
One challenge I encountered was choosing the right colors, fonts, and design elements that would represent my personality while keeping the overall design organized and visually appealing. I addressed this by exploring different design ideas, experimenting with various combinations, and selecting elements that best reflected my identity. Through this process, I learned to balance creativity with simplicity and consistency.
</p>

**◆ How I Could Apply or Improve This in the Future**

<p align="justify">
In the future, I can apply these skills when creating professional portfolios, resumes, presentations, and other materials that represent my personal and professional identity. As a future nurse, I can also use personal branding to establish a professional image, build confidence, and communicate my values and strengths. I will continue improving my design skills and learning how to present myself effectively in both academic and professional settings.
</p>
