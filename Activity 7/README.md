## 📘 **Activity 07: Resume Generation Using Artificial Intelligence** <p align="justify">


<p align="justify">
This activity focuses on using and comparing different artificial intelligence tools to generate and improve a professional resume. It involves organizing personal information, educational background, skills, achievements, and experiences into a structured and professional document. Through this activity, I explored how AI can assist in writing, editing, and formatting resumes while understanding the importance of presenting accurate and relevant information.
</p>

## ✨ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

<p align="justify">
Through this activity, I learned how artificial intelligence can make resume creation easier by helping organize information, improve sentence construction, and provide suggestions for professional formatting. I realized that a resume is an important document because it introduces my qualifications, skills, educational background, and experiences to potential employers. I also learned that although AI can assist in generating content, it is still important to review and personalize the information to ensure that everything accurately represents my abilities and achievements.
</p>

**◆ Key Concepts and Skills I Gained**

<p align="justify">
I gained knowledge about professional resume writing, proper formatting, and the organization of personal and academic information. I also developed my skills in using AI tools to generate, edit, and improve written content. Through comparing different AI-generated outputs, I learned how to evaluate which suggestions were more appropriate, professional, and relevant. Most importantly, I understood the importance of accuracy, clarity, and honesty when presenting my qualifications.
</p>

**◆ Challenges I Faced and How I Solved Them**

<p align="justify">
One challenge I encountered was ensuring that the AI-generated resume accurately reflected my personal information, educational background, skills, and experiences. Some generated suggestions required editing because they were too general or did not completely match my qualifications. I addressed this by reviewing the generated content, correcting inaccurate details, and selecting only the information that was relevant to my background. This helped me understand that AI should serve as a guide in creating a resume rather than completely replacing my own judgment.
</p>

**◆ How I Could Apply or Improve This in the Future**

<p align="justify">
In the future, I can apply these skills when preparing my resume for nursing applications, hospital employment, internships, and other professional opportunities. As a future registered nurse, I can use my resume to highlight my nursing education, clinical experiences, relevant skills, training, and achievements. I will continue improving my professional writing and digital skills to create a well-organized and updated resume that effectively represents my qualifications and readiness for my future career.
</p>
