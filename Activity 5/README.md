## 📘 **Activity 05: Artificial Intelligence Tools – AI Image Generation and Prompt Engineering** <p align="justify">

<p align="justify">
This activity focuses on exploring artificial intelligence tools, particularly AI-powered image generation, by creating images through written prompts and instructions. It involves understanding how artificial intelligence interprets user input and transforms descriptions into visual outputs. Through this activity, I was able to experiment with different prompts, compare generated results, and understand how detailed instructions can influence the quality, accuracy, and appearance of AI-generated images.
</p>

## ✨ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

<p align="justify">
Through this activity, I learned how artificial intelligence can be used as a creative tool to generate images and transform ideas into visual representations. I discovered that the quality of an AI-generated image depends greatly on the clarity and specificity of the prompt provided. I also realized that AI is useful in saving time, supporting creativity, and providing new ways to complete digital tasks. However, its generated outputs still need to be reviewed to ensure that they meet the intended purpose.
</p>

**◆ Key Concepts and Skills I Gained**

<p align="justify">
I gained knowledge about artificial intelligence, image generation, and prompt engineering. I learned how to construct clear and detailed prompts by specifying the subject, appearance, background, style, and other important visual elements. I also developed my critical thinking and creativity by comparing generated images and identifying how changes in instructions can affect the final results. Additionally, I learned the importance of using AI responsibly and treating it as a supportive tool rather than relying on it completely.
</p>

**◆ Challenges I Faced and How I Solved Them**

<p align="justify">
One challenge I encountered was generating images that accurately matched my expectations because some outputs did not completely follow the instructions I provided. I addressed this by improving my prompts, adding more specific descriptions, and adjusting the instructions to make my desired results clearer. I also compared different generated outputs to determine which prompt produced the most appropriate image. Through this process, I understood that effective communication with AI requires clear instructions, patience, and continuous experimentation.
</p>

**◆ How I Could Apply or Improve This in the Future**

<p align="justify">
In the future, I can apply these skills when creating educational illustrations, presentation materials, infographics, and other visual resources. As a nursing student, I can explore AI tools to support the development of health education materials and visual explanations of medical concepts, while ensuring that all health-related information is verified using reliable sources. I will continue improving my prompt-writing skills and learning how to use artificial intelligence ethically, responsibly, and effectively in my academic activities and future nursing profession.
</p>
