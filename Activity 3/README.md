## 📘 ABOUT THIS ACTIVITY

This activity focuses on creating social media infographics and organizing project documentation. It highlights the importance of visual communication, creativity, and proper presentation of information.

💡 WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

Key Concepts & Skills: I learned how to create informative and visually appealing infographics by combining text, images, colors, and other design elements. I also developed skills in organizing information, choosing appropriate layouts, and preparing clear and structured project documentation.

Challenges & Solutions: One challenge I encountered was deciding how to present information without making the infographic too crowded or difficult to understand. I addressed this by selecting only the most important details, using simple designs, and arranging the content in a clear and organized manner.

Future Applications: I can apply these skills when creating educational materials, social media content, and project reports. As a nursing student, I can also use infographics to share health information, promote health awareness, and educate communities through simple and engaging visual presentations.
