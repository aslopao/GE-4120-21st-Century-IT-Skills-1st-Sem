## 📘 **Activity 06: Website Development**


<p align="justify">
This activity focuses on designing and developing a personal website using digital tools to create an organized and accessible online platform. It involves learning how to arrange website content, customize layouts, incorporate images and other visual elements, and present information in a clear and professional manner. Through this activity, I explored the basic concepts of website development and learned how websites can be used to showcase personal information, projects, activities, and achievements.
</p>

## ✨ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

<p align="justify">
Through this activity, I learned the basic concepts of website development and how digital tools can help transform ideas into an actual online platform. I discovered the importance of organizing website content, choosing appropriate designs, and maintaining a consistent layout to make a website visually appealing and easy to navigate. I also realized that a personal website can serve as a digital portfolio where I can showcase my academic activities, projects, skills, and accomplishments.
</p>

**◆ Key Concepts and Skills I Gained**

<p align="justify">
I gained knowledge about basic website design, content organization, layout customization, and the use of digital tools in creating an online portfolio. I developed my creativity and learned how to arrange text, images, links, and other website elements to make the content organized and accessible. I also gained a better understanding of the importance of user-friendly navigation, consistency, and visual presentation in website development.
</p>

**◆ Challenges I Faced and How I Solved Them**

<p align="justify">
One challenge I encountered was organizing the website elements and making sure that the content was properly arranged and displayed. I also found it challenging to choose suitable layouts and designs that would make the website both attractive and easy to navigate. I addressed these challenges by exploring the available tools, experimenting with different layouts, reviewing the website's appearance, and making necessary adjustments to improve its overall structure and presentation.
</p>

**◆ How I Could Apply or Improve This in the Future**

<p align="justify">
In the future, I can apply these skills by continuously updating my personal website to showcase my academic achievements, nursing experiences, projects, certificates, and professional development. As a future nurse, I can also explore creating websites that provide educational information about health promotion, disease prevention, and community health awareness. I will continue improving my digital literacy and website design skills to create more organized, informative, and accessible online platforms that can be useful in both my academic journey and future nursing profession.
</p>
